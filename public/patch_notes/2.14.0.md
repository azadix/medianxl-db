## Endgame Quests
### Changes to late endgame monsters
* The below changes aim to improve the balance between gear requirements for different damage sources
	* They only apply to enemies in: Heroic Rifts 133+, Sescheron's Destroyer, Samael, Deimoss, Heart of Darkness
* Removed seasonal absorb rotation from Heroic Rifts
* Monsters no longer use player percentage Absorb stat against Fire, Cold, and Lightning damage types
* Fire/Cold/Lightning/Poison damage with maxed elemental pierce will now result in up to 2.5x more damage than physical
	* Elements should deal more final damage than physical because of the need to stack pierce
	* However, the amount of this out-performance was not standardized and could reach between (3-4)x on some enemies
	* This made elemental builds disproportionately strong and reduced gearing requirements too much against these enemies
* Magic damage will now result in up to 1.2x more final damage than physical
* See the respective sections below for more details

# Characters
## Main
* Activation Frequency:
	* All periodic skills will now dynamically display their Activation Delay, making it easier to see the scaling
			* This will be displayed in seconds for skills with longer delays and frames for skills with a base delay around 1 second
	* Activation Frequency Efficiency is now listed under the synergies section for affected skills as "Activation Frequency Multiplier"
	* Fire/Cold/Lightning/Poison only sources no longer exist. This change is not retroactive to the Elder Law and split Elemental Band Sacred uniques
	* Removed jewel affix
* Summons:
	* Changed how summon resistances work
	* Before: get 75% elemental resistance from Skill Levels, up to 75% from Minion Resistance stats, -100% Hell penalty; resulting in up to 50% max resistance
	* Now: up to 105% from Minion Resistance stats, -30% Hell penalty; resulting in up to 75% max elemental resistances
	* The goal is to simplify the formula, require slightly more Minion Resistance itemization but summons take half elemental damage compared to before
	* Dimensional Labyrinth 9+ enemy elemental pierce modifier has also been changed (see above)
	* Magic resistance penalty is now -30% on Nightmare, -60% on Hell; down from -50% and -100%
	* New affix: Summon Physical Resist. Works like the player variant and cannot go over 50% max physical resist
	* Changed how summoning works
	* Before: each cast would summon 1 minion, this could be increased slightly via certain items or skill mechanics
	* Now: each cast will summon the maximum number of minions on the skill, summon cast items and mechanics will be reworked/removed
	* Each cast will now have a dynamic cooldown, based on the number of summons created and their strength
			* For regular units (e.g. {{Blood Skeleton}}) 1.2 seconds per minion, for strong units (e.g. {{Veil King}}) 20 seconds per minion, for temporary units (e.g. Hawks) 0.4 seconds per minion
			* The cooldown only applies to the respective skill. E.g. casting {{Blood Skeleton}} won't block your from casting {{Abyss Knight}} straight away
	* Each cast will now have a dynamic mana cost, based on the number of summons created and their strength, capped at 50% of Maximum Mana
			* For regular units it will be 6% of Maximum Mana per minion, for strong units 25% of Maximum Mana per minion, for temporary units 2% of Maximum Mana per minion
			* Mana cost will be 1 in town
	* The above changes will only apply totraditional summoner abilities and will not affect skills like: Totems, Golem, {{Summon Edyrems}}, {{Resurrect}}, {{Summon Familiars}}
	* The goals of the above changes:
			* Make resummoning your army faster after death and on entering a new game
			* Give summoners more burst damage. Even if the army gets wiped out soon after, this will be more impactful than chain casting 1 unit at a time
			* Make summoner gameplay more tactical and increase the importance of minion defensive stats, as you can no longer constantly generate units
			* Differentiate stat priority from casters a bit. Cast speed will now be mostly useless for summoners, maximum Mana won't be beneficial to sustain and you will need to rely on Mana Regeneration and reduced Mana Cost of Skills
	* Added 25% chance to avoid damage to minions affected by the above (excluding {{Veil King}}, {{Bear Companion}}, {{Fire Elementals}}, {{Harvesters}}), reduced base life by 20%
	* This is a small effective health boost for regular gameplay but will help against complete army wipe out boss skills
	* Summoned Minion Damage/Life/Attack Rating strings on items are now "Summon Damage/Life/Attack Rating"
	* Summoned Minion Resistances strings on items are now "Summon Elemental Resistances"
* Changes to Mana sustain options:
	* Mana on Striking: removed from items
	* This stat was impossible to balance in the context of Median's high hit count skills. Even low amounts of it would eliminate all Mana costs, and if skill Mana costs were balanced around it - they would become prohibitively high for characters without the stat
	* Mana Leech: removed from items
	* The problem with this stat is that it is effective against multiple targets but useless against few and single targets. Its efficiency also diminishes further at high area levels as monsters receive reduced damage (in order to bypass Diablo 2 unit life limits). Finally, it is affected by a very high internal divisor on Hell difficulty that is not communicated in game
	* Mana Cost of Skills: new stat
	* Reduces (or increases) the Mana cost of all active skills by a percentage
	* Does not apply to the base Mana cost of a skill, in order to allow us to have skills exempt from the mechanic if we wish
	* Will replace most sources of Mana on Striking and Mana Leech on items, except non-customizable charms - we want Mana management to be an active gear choice
	* This stat has the following benefits:
			* Works equally well in all combat situations: multiple, few, single target; low and high area levels
			* Simple effect, easy to understand without any documentation
			* Rewards stacking, instead of eliminating the entire Mana system for most Weapon Damage builds with 1 affix
			* Also works for casters, not just Weapon Damage builds
