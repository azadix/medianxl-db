# Game
## General
* Added Damage to Demons and Damage to Undead stat totals to the second page of Character Screen
* Fire, Cold, Lightning and Physical Thorns now return their damage to melee attacker even if the attack misses or gets blocked or dodged
* Fire, Lightning and Physical Thorns now also return their damage to ranged attackers - but only when those land an actual hit on you
* Fixed an issue where Attack Speeds over 255 were not giving any benefits in calculations (e.g. Facebreaker)
## Engine
* Fixed show loot interfering with player movement
* Integrated D2FPS functionality: less CPU usage, unlocked render frame rate, motion smoothing
## Experience
* Experience required for levels 100-120 increased by 10%
* The early endgame stage progresses too quickly which results in insufficient upgrades found to prepare characters for harder challenges
* The level requirement to enter certain dungeons along the campaign areas has been removed (see Dungeons section)
## Monsters
* Monsters using the skill {{Gorefest}} now use a monster specific version
